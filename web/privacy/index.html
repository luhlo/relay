Relay privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · Operated by luhlo

Relay helps parents and caregivers coordinate focus time, childcare, and handoffs. Here is how the Android app and relay.luhlo.com handle your information.

Information you provide

  • Your account: your email address and account identifier. Email/password and Google sign-in are handled by Supabase. If you choose Google, Supabase may also receive basic profile information Google provides, such as your name and profile image. Relay does not ask to read your Gmail messages.
  • Your household: display names, a family name, the number of children and caregivers, child names or aliases, and the emails and roles of people you invite. Child names are optional; blank names become Baby 1, Baby 2, and so on.
  • Your records: scheduled shifts; focus and childcare sessions; selected child labels; start, end, and interruption times; timer check-ins; and automatic stop reasons. Your Insights are calculated from these records.
  • Your settings: reminders, widget colors, and the optional personal-time agreement.

Local records and cloud records

Device-only records are stored in Relay’s database on Android or in your browser’s local storage on the web. They are copied into a cloud account only when you choose to import them.

When signed in, your account and household records are stored using Supabase. Cloud tracking requires a connection. The app may contact authentication services to check a saved sign-in session, and the website’s hosting service receives requests when you load its pages.

How Relay uses information

Relay uses this information to sign you in, save your history, operate timers and widgets, calculate Insights, manage household invitations, send account-related emails, and provide the reminders you enable. The current app does not include advertising or marketing analytics SDKs.

Who can see your records

Members of your household can view household details, members’ display names and roles, and each member’s recorded time and Insights. Only invite or join people you intend to share this information with. Each member tracks their own sessions.

Supabase supplies authentication and cloud storage. Resend delivers account-related emails through our authentication email service. GitHub Pages hosts the website. Google processes sign-in when you choose Google. Authorized operators can access service data as needed to maintain Relay and help with account or security issues.

When these services process requests, they may receive technical information such as IP addresses and request details. Their privacy information is available from Supabase, Resend, GitHub, and Google.

Google Calendar

If you select Add to Google Calendar, Relay opens an event draft with your shift type and times. You choose whether to save it. Relay does not read your calendar or automatically synchronize calendar changes.

Android permissions and widgets

Notifications provide shift reminders, timer check-ins, and automatic-stop notices when you allow them. Alarms & reminders access allows more precise timing. Android may defer alerts when permissions are off or system restrictions apply. Relay restores scheduled checks after a restart.

The app does not request access to your device’s contacts, microphone, camera, or location. A placed widget can show child labels and timer details on your home screen. You can use aliases if you prefer not to display names there.

Security

Relay connects to cloud services over HTTPS. Sign-in and database access rules restrict account and household data to authorized users. Your device stores a sign-in session so you can stay signed in. Protect access to your device, especially if it is shared. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and account deletion

Cloud records are kept to provide your account and history until you delete your account. You can start deletion from the app’s Account settings or from the Delete your Relay account page. The website works even if the Android app is no longer installed.

Deleting your account removes your sign-in account, membership, personal saved schedules and time records, and invitations associated with your account. It does not delete other household members’ accounts or their own records.

If you are the household owner and other members remain, ownership transfers to the earliest-joined remaining member when another owner is needed. Shared household information, including child labels, stays available to remaining members. If you are the last member, the household and its shared information are deleted too.

Separately created device-only records remain on the device. On Android, you can remove them through Settings → Apps → Relay → Storage → Clear data. On the web, clear stored site data for relay.luhlo.com in your browser. These actions also remove locally saved sign-in sessions and preferences. Uninstalling Relay does not delete cloud records.

Service-provider backups, delivery logs, or security records may persist under the provider’s retention rules and security or legal obligations. Account deletion removes your records from Relay’s active application database; it does not promise immediate removal from every provider backup or log.

Information about children

Relay is intended for adult parents and caregivers, rather than children creating accounts or using the app directly. Adults can enter child names or use generic labels. Relay does not ask for children’s dates of birth, photos, locations, or contact details.

To ask about information entered about a child, contact luhlostudios@gmail.com.
