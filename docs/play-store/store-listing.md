## Short description (80-character limit)
Manage focus time, baby care, and shared schedules for new parents.

## Full description
Work, care, and everything in between. Relay helps parents and caregivers coordinate their day and see where their time goes.

START WITH ONE TAP
Start Focus for personal or work time, or choose Baby Time and select a child or all your children. Large buttons make it easier to track time while your hands are full.

MAKE INTERRUPTIONS VISIBLE
Tap Interruption when focus time is interrupted. A separate stopwatch tracks the interruption while actual focus time pauses. Resume when you are ready, and see total time, uninterrupted focus, and interrupted time together.

PLAN THE HANDOFF
Create daily shift blocks, adjust their times, or push a block by 15 minutes when plans change. Optional Android reminders notify you before a shift ends and at the handoff. You can also open a shift as a Google Calendar draft to save yourself.

SEE YOUR SHARED RHYTHM
Review daily totals, childcare time, focus time, and interruption history. Compare trends with line charts and time breakdowns, browse previous days, or select your own date range.

BRING YOUR HOUSEHOLD TOGETHER
Create a household, choose child names or use Baby 1, Baby 2, and so on, and invite parents or caregivers. Each member tracks their own sessions and can view household members' Insights.

CONTROL TIME FROM YOUR HOME SCREEN
Add Relay's resizable 2 × 1 or 4 × 1 Android widget. Start work or childcare, track interruptions, resume, and end sessions from the widget. Customize its colors in Settings.

CHOOSE HOW TO SAVE
Use local records on your device, or sign in with email or Google to save with your account. Signed-in tracking requires an internet connection. Local history can be copied into an empty account when you choose.

KEEP TIMERS FROM RUNNING FORGOTTEN
Optional check-in notifications ask whether you are still tracking. Relay records automatic stop times for unanswered check-ins and long-running sessions. Notification delivery depends on your Android permissions and system settings.
