Account controls

Delete your Relay account

Delete your account and its saved records from this website. You do not need the Android app installed.

Start with your own account

Sign in with the email or Google account you use for Relay. Review the details, then type DELETE and confirm. Deletion is permanent.

Sign in to delete my account

Signing in opens the review step. It does not delete anything by itself.

What is deleted?

  • Your Relay sign-in account and personal household membership.
  • Your saved schedules, focus and childcare records, interruption history, check-ins, and account preferences.
  • Invitations associated with your account.

What happens to my family’s records?

Other members keep their accounts and their own history. If you own the household and another owner is needed, ownership transfers to the earliest-joined remaining member. Shared information, such as the family name and child labels, remains for those members.

If you are the last member, Relay also deletes the household and its shared information.

What about device-only records?

Local records created separately from your cloud account stay on the device where you used them. To remove them:

  • Android: open Settings → Apps → Relay → Storage → Clear data.
  • Web: use your browser’s controls to clear stored site data for relay.luhlo.com.

Clearing device or browser storage also removes saved sign-in sessions and preferences on that device. Uninstalling Relay does not delete your cloud account.

Backups and service records

Deletion removes your records from Relay’s active application database. Provider backups, email-delivery logs, or security records may persist under provider retention rules and security or legal obligations. See our privacy policy for more information.

Need help signing in?

Use Forgot password on Relay’s sign-in page, or email luhlostudios@gmail.com. We verify account ownership before acting on a deletion request.
